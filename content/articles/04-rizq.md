> [20:132](https://quran.com/20/132) And **enjoin prayer upon your family and be steadfast therein**. We ask you not for provision; We provide for you, and the outcome is for righteousness.

> [71:10-12](https://quran.com/71/10-12) 
> And said, **Ask forgiveness of your Lord**. Indeed, He is Most Forgiving.
> He will send down the sky upon you (in) abundant
> And Provide you with wealth and children, and give you gardens and rivers.

> [65:2-3](https://quran.com/65/2-3)
> ... That is instructed to whoever should **believe in Allāh and the Last Day. And whoever fears Allāh** - He will make for him a way out
> And will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allāh - then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allāh will accomplish His purpose. Indeen Allah has set for everything a measure

> [11:10-11](https://quran.com/11/10-11)
> But if We give him a taste of favor after hardship has touched him, he will surely say, "Bad times have left me." Indeed, he is **prideful** and **boastful**
> Except for those who are **patient and do righteous deeds**; those will have forgiveness and great reward.

> [2:261](https://quran.com/2/261)
The example of those who **spend their wealth in the way of Allāh** is like a seed which grows seven spikes; in each spike is a hundred grains. And Allāh multiplies for whom He wills. And Allāh is all-Encompassing and Knowing.

> [57:18](https://quran.com/57/18)
> Indeed, **those men and women who give in charity and lend to Allah a good loan** will have it multiplied for them, and they will have an honourable reward.

> [14:7](https://quran.com/14/7)
> And when your Lord proclaimed, ‘If you are **grateful**, I will certainly give you more. But if you are **ungrateful**, surely My punishment is severe.’

> [67:15](https://quran.com/67/15)
> It is He who made the earth tame for you - so walk among its slopes and eat of His provision - and to Him is the resurrection.
